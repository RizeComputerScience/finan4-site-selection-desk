Check Site Selection Desk receipts

This page is for instructors. Add one or more receipt files (PNG) that students uploaded. For each one, the page rebuilds that student's numbers from the email on the receipt and re-grades every card from scratch. It does not trust the score printed on the image.

Suggested points: 20 points times verified cards divided by 8. Nothing leaves your computer; the files are read in this browser only.
